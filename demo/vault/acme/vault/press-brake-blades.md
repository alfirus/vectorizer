## Maintenance schedule

The Acme 150T press brake on line 3 needs blade replacement every **4000
cycles**. The counter resets after each replacement and is logged in the
shift handover notes.

## Replacement procedure

1. Lock out the hydraulic supply and verify zero pressure.
2. Remove the upper beam tooling, working outside-in.
3. Fit the replacement blade set, torque to 180 Nm in a star pattern.
4. Run three test bends on scrap and measure against the tolerance card.

## Parts

- Replacement blade set: part `ACME-BLADE-150T`
- Torque spec: 180 Nm
- Tolerance card: kept at the line-3 QC station
