# Acme Line 3 — Safety Checklist (SAMPLE DATA)

> **Synthetic sample** shipped with the Vectorizer demo. Fictional company,
> fictional machines — safe to index, search, and delete.

## Before every shift

- Confirm the light curtain on the press brake is aligned (green LEDs).
- Check the two-hand control buttons both latch before the ram moves.
- Verify the fire extinguisher at column C4 is charged and accessible.

## Weekly

- Test the emergency stop chain end-to-end and log the result.
- Inspect lifting slings for cuts; retire anything with exposed core.
- Review the near-miss board with the shift lead.

## Reporting

Near misses go to the shift lead the same day. No blame, no exceptions —
the checklist only works if reporting is frictionless.
